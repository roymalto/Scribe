SCRIBE - for one PC

Scribe runs on this PC only. Nothing is sent over the internet: your patients and prescriptions stay here.

OPENING SCRIBE
  Double-click the Scribe icon on the desktop (or Start menu > Scribe > Scribe). Scribe opens in your web browser.
  The very first time, it takes about a minute and then asks for your clinic's name, your name as printed on
  prescriptions, your PRC licence number, and an email and password to sign in with.
  Write the password down somewhere safe: nobody else can reset it on this PC.

  Scribe keeps running in the background until you shut down the PC. To close it yourself:
  Start menu > Scribe > Stop Scribe.

BACKUPS
  Each day you open Scribe, it makes a backup in Documents\Scribe Backups (the last 30 are kept).
  Copy that folder to a USB drive or cloud storage now and then: if this PC breaks or is stolen, the backups on it
  go with it.
  Start menu > Scribe > Back up now          makes one straight away.
  Start menu > Scribe > Restore a backup     puts Scribe back the way it was on the day of a backup.

PRESCRIPTIONS
  Download PDF saves the prescription as a file (in your Downloads folder) to print or send by Viber, Messenger or
  email. "PDF to sign by hand" leaves your signature image off, to print and sign with a pen.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  Scribe shows a message and keeps a log in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Scribe\logs (paste that address into File Explorer).
  Send launcher.log to whoever helps you with Scribe.

UPDATING OR REMOVING
  Run a newer Scribe-Setup file to update: your data stays.
  Removing Scribe (Settings > Apps) also leaves your data and backups on the PC.
